Privilo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Privacy at a glance: Privilo is designed so that Safari browsing activity stays on your iPhone. Privilo does not collect, store or upload the websites you visit, your browsing history, or the blocking activity generated by Safari. Purchase and subscription information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat to provide Privilo Pro.

1. About Privilo

Privilo is an iOS application that protects Safari by blocking ads, trackers, cookie notices and other unwanted web content. Privilo also includes privacy education and an optional Privacy Checkup feature.

2. Browsing and blocking data

Privilo's Safari content-blocking rules are applied by Safari on your device. Safari does not provide Privilo with the pages you visit or the network requests made by websites.

Privilo therefore does not collect or upload your browsing history, visited URLs, DNS queries, page contents, blocked URLs or the websites on which blocking occurs.

Privilo does not use browsing activity for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Information stored on your iPhone

Privilo stores the settings needed to operate the app and its Safari extension on your device. This can include whether protection is enabled, filter-list preferences, websites you have allowed or chosen to block, the installed filter version and date, and a short local activity history such as protection changes and filter updates.

This information is kept in local App Group storage shared by the Privilo app and its Safari extension. It is not uploaded to a Privilo server. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's normal device and backup behaviour.

Privilo's diagnostics report is generated locally and is designed not to contain websites or browsing history.

4. Privacy Checkup

Privacy Checkup explains iOS privacy controls and helps you review access to resources such as the microphone, camera and location.

Privilo does not request access to your microphone, camera or location, and iOS does not allow Privilo to inspect the current permissions or sensor activity of other apps directly.

Privacy Checkup may optionally import an App Privacy Report that you choose to export from iOS Settings and select in the Files picker. The imported report is parsed on your device and used to show information such as resource access reported by iOS and contacts with domains that match Privilo's known ad and tracker lists.

The imported report and the resulting analysis are kept in memory only. Privilo does not save the report to its own storage, upload it to a server or share it with Privilo, RevenueCat or another third party.

Importing an App Privacy Report does not mean that Privilo itself performed or blocked the activity shown in the report. The report describes activity recorded by iOS for other apps.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Privilo Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payments and may process purchase information under Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Privilo can use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscription and entitlement status. RevenueCat may process information needed to identify and verify purchases, such as App Store purchase or subscription records, product information, purchase and renewal dates, prices, renewal status and an anonymous App User ID generated for the app.

The Privilo app does not provide RevenueCat with your browsing history, visited websites, blocking rules, allowlist, blocklist or imported App Privacy Report.

Privilo does not use RevenueCat for advertising, cross-app tracking or behavioural profiling. Privilo does not create a customer account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address or Apple Account credentials to Privilo.

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Information we do not collect

Privilo does not require an account and does not intentionally collect or maintain a server-side database containing your browsing history, visited websites, page contents, blocked URLs, allowlisted websites, custom blocked websites, filter preferences, protection activity, microphone recordings, camera images or location history.

Privilo contains no advertising SDK and does not use advertising identifiers or App Tracking Transparency for its core app functionality.

7. Data retention

Operational settings and the local protection activity history remain on your iPhone until you change or clear them, or remove the app. Imported App Privacy Reports are kept only in memory during the current Privilo session and can be cleared from the Privacy Checkup.

Purchase and subscription information handled by Apple or RevenueCat is retained by those services according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

8. Sharing and third parties

Privilo does not sell your personal information and does not share browsing activity with advertisers or data brokers.

The principal third-party services involved in the app are Apple, for iOS, Safari and App Store purchases, and RevenueCat, when enabled in the build, for subscription and entitlement management. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Security

Privilo is designed around on-device processing so that browsing-related information does not need to be transmitted to a Privilo backend. The app also avoids sending imported App Privacy Reports, website lists or blocking activity to a server.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but Privilo is designed to minimise the amount of sensitive information that leaves the device.

10. Children's privacy

Privilo is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Privilo.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

You control Privilo's access to your device through iOS settings and control which websites you allow or block within the app. You can turn protection off, clear the local activity history and clear an imported App Privacy Report from within Privilo.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing of personal information. Where information is processed by Apple or RevenueCat, those requests may need to be made to the relevant service under its own privacy procedures.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Privilo's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Privilo, please use the support contact published with the Privilo App Store listing.
